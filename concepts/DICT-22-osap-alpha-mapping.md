# DICT-22 · OSAP alpha-catalog mapping (Chen–Zimmermann openassetpricing.com)
The dictionary audited against the Open Source Asset Pricing catalog: 212 published cross-sectional predictors, each mapped to a disposition + concept expression. The per-predictor map is data: `mappings/osap_map.csv` (generated by `mappings/build_osap_map.py` from the upstream `SignalDoc.csv`, vintage 2025-09). This is the coverage stress test the dictionary was built for: any catalog alpha we cannot express reveals a missing concept, a missing recipe primitive, or a missing data source — and the map says which.

## 2. What the mapping confirmed
- The accounting core is complete: 99 Accounting predictors → 0 blocked; every Compustat item OSAP uses has a DICT-01 concept or a §4 addition. Piotroski, Ohlson, GP, NOA, accruals families are direct hits — the v1 design guess was right.
- Price/Trading nearly complete: 58 predictors, 1 blocked (PIN, intraday). Gaps are parameter variants (window lengths) — argues for parameterized concept variants, not new concepts.
- The Analyst block reproduces the DICT-15 §3 finding independently: 9 of 18 blocked on exactly the revision-level estimates gate. The catalog now quantifies what that vendor is worth: ~13 published predictors unlock at once.
- 13F/link domains: the framework (own.*, link.exposure) was the right abstraction; what's missing is population depth (holder-level history, customer-supplier links), not schema.
- Options: DICT-18's surface concepts cover 7 of 9 directly; the 2 misses are option VOLUME (added §4) — a domain we'd modeled as prices-only.

## 3. Recipe primitives (C2 vocabulary the 59 ready-recipe rows require; recipes stay recipes — not concepts — per v1 governance)
cross_sectional_residual(y, xs, by?) · industry_adjust(x, scheme, level) · double_sort(a, b, q) · seasonal_window(ret, months_offset_set) · streak(x, cond) · rank_weighted_mean · rolling_regression(y, x, w) · hhi(x, by)
Each primitive versioned like extraction recipes (DICT-07 rules); a catalog alpha implemented = signal concept whose `formula` composes primitives over concept_ids, with provenance `{osap_acronym, authors, year, sample}` — the citation travels into lineage.

## 6. Governance of catalog imports
Adopting a predictor = new signal concept referencing the OSAP acronym in provenance; OSAP's own SignalDoc vintage is pinned (`mappings/osap_signaldoc_2025.csv`); upstream catalog updates re-run the map build and diff dispositions (a predictor moving ready→blocked signals a concept regression). OSAP data itself (their published returns/portfolios) is NOT ingested as signal values — we re-derive from our own data; their numbers serve only as X2-style replication benchmarks (tolerance documented per predictor sample window).
